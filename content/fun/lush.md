I've always had problems with my skin. I tried product after product, but my skin got worse not better. So when our partner (we really need a nickname at this point my god) suggested we try a cleanser from Lush I wasn't too excited. It surely wouldn't work. Then an employee semi-forced me to try out some products. I caved and bought them all, lol. My wallet hurts but... what's that MY SKIN IS CLEAR? Okay now I need everything from Lush.

## Cleanser

The Angels on Bare Skin cleanser is AMAZING.
I also bought 9-5 solely for removing makeup. Works well! Smell is interesting but not bad. 

## Hair

My latest experiment is hair care. I got a shampoo bar (I forgot which one, something with honey I believe) and tried it for the first time yesterday. I also used the Power (?) conditioner. I love the scent, it kind of reminds me of the perfume our partner wore on our first date.
My hair is not as smooth and silky as with regular shampoo and conditioner. But to be fair it's all natural products without silicone (I believe?) and usually I'd put in some hair oil with silicone because I really need my hair to be extra silky. And it's the first time. So I'm not giving up on Lush yet.

## Lotions

Sleepy body lotion. Need I say more?

The flaxseed hand cream is also decent. Tbh I almost threw not (not being dramatic) the first time I smelled it. It is that bad. But my hands are soft, so.
When I don't feel like being assaulted by the smell of my hand cream I like "Handy Gurugu".
