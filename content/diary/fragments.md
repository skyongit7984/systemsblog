The last days were crazy. We had an intense therapy session and followed by an equally intense discussion with our partner and -- something profound changed. Up until now our Little One carried a big amount of trauma memories, fear and ocd. (See our post about doubt) And suddenly those… didn’t belong to her anymore? They (= the memories and emotions) floated around in our mind as a fragment, belonging to no one. This was and is a profound change in our internal structure and selves understanding. Our Little One (and in extension we all) battled with these memories and emotions for so long and so intensely, and we thought those we're a part of her. And now we find out they are a fragment that can attach to anyone basically, and now she can just be. And sleep. And get the rest she deserves. 


Well that fragment isn’t gone though, as I said. I am still tending to it like I did to our Little One when the frgament was still attached to her. 

I think it is a great sign that that fragment split itself off. Our psyche felt safe enough to let go of it to an extent. 

I am calling it a fragment because it has no sense of self, mostly communicates through images, feeling and only rudimentary language and because it only contains a certain set of memories and feelings.

The way I have to handle this fragment is similar but also quite different than when it was still attached to out Little One. I still have to soothe it and care for it. But it no longer plagues one of us like a demon, like miasma. (Any Zelda players here?) 

I can now communicate with it directly instead of having to go through a middle man. 
Through this direct access it was able to show me its core, the main traumatic stuff it carries.

We'll see how things develop and how this will affect our system. I'll keep you updated. :))
