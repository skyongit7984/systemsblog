Sometimes, I can hear the pillars of my psyche aching. When I follow a dangerous thought. When I allow us to feel and the emotions become too intense. I can feel the foundational building blocks aching under pressure,
and that's when we run, run, run, back to where we started, back to sanity. What happens when everything collapses under the weight? We don't know. Maybe psychosis. Maybe we will die. At least it feels like we might.
Maybe nothing and we will be set free from the past? I don't know. But this whole trauma healing journey has led me to so many places deep in my psyche that I feel like I touched the molecular structure of the 
universe, what everything's made of. I don't like how fragile and sensitive it is.
