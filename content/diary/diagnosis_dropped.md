Just got back from a therapy session. So guess the therapist agrees we have a complex dissociative disorder. Tbh I kind of expected this moment to be bigger than it actually was. I knew beforehand nothing would change but a tiny part of me still hoped the validation from outside would help. I mean it’s nice someone else sees what I see, but it really *really* didn‘t change anything. 

Also, I think I’m just better off handling all the plural stuff on my own. I believe she hinted at fusion as the only viable treatment, and NO. I am happy we are all in a much better headspace than just a few months ago, because we wouldn‘t have handled that well.

Of course it is a little worrying to know your therapist believes it is best to steer you all in the direction of fusion. What I learned the hard way though is that it’s wise to never trust a therapist or doctor 100%. This is just personal experience. I am not trying to influence anyone reading. It’s just what I have come to realize for myself. It is very much a „take the good, leave the rest“ kind of situation.

This is turning into a rant lol but it‘s frustrating to always have to put up with bullshit from “professionals“. I am mostly happy with my current one. I appreciate that she has great intuition and can actually empathize with her patients (not all therapists do that, actually), and she believes in DID. It’s insane I have to list that as a pro, because even just finding a therapist that ticks that box was hard. 
But of course she had to be the fusion kind. There’s always ***something*** with therapists.
